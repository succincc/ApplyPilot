Run a migration against the connected Supabase project that adds these columns

2. SUBMISSION VERIFICATION UI

Applications now carry a confirmation_status on the jobs table: 'confirmed'
(the employer emailed back, so the submission definitely landed), 'pending'
(applied recently, too early to expect a reply), or 'unconfirmed' (applied over
48 hours ago with total silence).

- On job cards and in the job detail drawer, show a small status dot on anything
  with status='applied' or later:
    green filled  = confirmed   (tooltip: "Employer acknowledged — submission verified")
    gray hollow   = pending     (tooltip: "Waiting on acknowledgement")
    amber outline = unconfirmed (tooltip: "No reply after 48h — this submission may not have gone through")
  In the drawer, show confirmed_at as a relative time when present.

- On the Dashboard, add a "Verified" counter next to the other counters:
  confirmed divided by total applied, as a percentage, with the raw numbers
  beneath it.

- On the Analytics page, add a "Confirmation rate by source" horizontal bar
  chart: for each site with at least 5 applications older than 48 hours, show
  the percentage with confirmation_status='confirmed'. Sort ascending so the
  worst source appears first. Color bars under 40% amber.

- Caption that chart exactly: "Some employers never acknowledge applications, so
  a low number is not proof of failure on its own. Compare sources — if one ATS
  confirms 80% and another confirms 5%, submissions to the second are probably
  failing silently."

This app only reads and writes Supabase. It must never scrape job boards, call
job APIs, or submit applications — a separate local engine does all of that.

================================================================================
WHAT TO EXPECT AFTERWARD
================================================================================

The new screens will look EMPTY and every status dot will be GRAY.

That is correct, not a bug. Those tables are filled by the engine running on
your PC. The panel is the window; the engine is what puts things in it.

They populate once you have run, on your computer:

    applypilot init      one-time setup: resume, profile, free Gemini API key
    applypilot doctor    every line should read OK
    applypilot verify    live-tests the job APIs, your email, and Supabase
    ./start.sh           starts the bridge; the panel's status pill turns green

Then prove one application end to end before turning up volume:

    applypilot run all
    applypilot apply --dry-run --limit 1

Drop --dry-run when the form looks right. The confirmation email arriving in
your inbox is the moment the machine is real.


================================================================================
IMPORTANT: ONE EMAIL ADDRESS
================================================================================

Two settings must be the SAME address:

  1. personal.email in profile.json   -> typed onto every application form
  2. MAIL_ADDRESS in .env             -> the inbox scanned for replies

If they differ, employer confirmations arrive at an inbox nothing is watching,
your Inbox screen stays empty, and nothing links up. 'applypilot doctor' now
checks this and fails loudly if they disagree.
